`PqxdhRecordFactsOriginPublicationEvent` proves three statements relating the frozen `UnpublishedAccept` event to an exact failed-writer seal witness in the initialized source. `ctxView_prefixes` transfers the complete source receive-prefix theorem to the public view's literal transcript. `forged_failed` combines the actual accepted receive's temporal witness with the frozen event's absence of earlier counterpart publication. Caller-known handles are excluded by the event's actual hidden-slot test; the sender is derived from the retained physical owner and opposite direction.

The resulting `FailedWriterAt` witness retains the literal successful receive, routed handle, installed slot, material coordinate, decoded frame, dictionary entry, actual plaintext and physical sender. Its complete frame satisfies `codec.frameWrite frame = none`. The proof compares frames by matching the same seal entry and preserves the event's original raw bytes and optional plaintext. It never inserts a recovered plaintext into a supplied target.

`ctxRun_unpublished_failed` applies this argument to the complete initialized adaptive source. It returns the same transcript decomposition and a failed-writer witness for that event, without an initial history, honest-target, prior-origin or chronology premise. The chronology comes from the before-receive dictionary and the actual prior transcript established in the preceding modules, rather than from final dictionary membership alone.

This is event inclusion, not the numerical publication bound. The witness retains its dictionary entry in the final state but does not select the earlier service-query ordinal at which it was sealed. Selecting that source query, projecting unused private writer logs, coupling the full continuation until its first match, and counting attempted matches remain required for the statistical result. Replay/random hidden modes and the existing `FrameReadBack` contract are the explicit scope. No stronger codec law, new primitive contract or query-cost waiver is added; no Rust, persistence or snapshot behavior changes.
