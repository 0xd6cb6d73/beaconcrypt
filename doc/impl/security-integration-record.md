## Preserved exclusions

Forward secrecy does not imply post-compromise security: a current symmetric chain compromise exposes its descendants, and retained skipped keys, snapshots, copied secrets and plaintext constrain past confidentiality. The 269 core normal-return certificates do not establish whole-application runtime or memory safety. Constant-time primitives and comparison do not prove constant-time protocol execution, and this work claims no demonstrated timing attack. A self-signed beacon authenticates its signer but does not authorize tasking; recipient policy remains the application's responsibility.
