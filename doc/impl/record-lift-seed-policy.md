# Adjacent chain policy for the persistent initial child

`PqxdhRecordLiftSeedPolicy` identifies the independent seed endpoint `seedUniform` of `PqxdhRecordLiftGlobalSeedCut.observed_bound` with the next concrete mixed-policy hybrid, in which the selected initial coordinate `(slot, direction, start direction)` is ideal. It is the seed-hop analogue of the published `PqxdhRecordLiftChildPolicy`, stage C1 of the chain track (item 5, takeover plan §2 S3). It also proves the generic supported-prefix lemmas that the chain hybrid uses for its terminal congruence.

`initial state` is the coordinate whose source input is the masked seed half; `reads_iff` shows that `GlobalSeedCut.Reads` holds exactly there, and `needs_lookup` and `needs_iff` that the relay of `PqxdhRecordLiftSeedRelay` requests its child exactly at the lookup of that coordinate (when it is real). `interpreter_fixed` shows that every seed-mask service keeps the selected slot, direction and retained opposite half. The state view `view hidden` is `CtxProtocol.erasePrivate ∘ GlobalSeedCut.realize hidden`: it restores the masked half, on the proof side only, and removes the native and uniform counters. `different_source` shows that every service other than the selected initial lookup has, after the view, the law of the bare source interpreter under the adjacent policy `Function.update ideal (initial state) true`. `lookup_child`, `relay_child`, `view_putChild`, `view_child` and `child_source` show that at the selected lookup the relay samples once and then reuses its cell, the real seed-mask lookup writes the supplied value into the table and charges its native KDF call, and after the view this equals the adjacent ideal lookup, for a first miss and for repeated hits. `relay_different`, `query_source`, `different_entry` and `query_invariant` complete the single-step picture: every relay service agrees with the adjacent policy on the counter-erased state, and the invariant `Good` (the initial coordinate is fixed, it is real under `ideal` (`Ready`), and the cell equals its table entry (`CellMatches`)) is preserved under every supported reply.

## Supported prefixes

The second part is generic over the private Protocol interpreter. `lawEq_bind_support` is law equality as a congruence on the support of the first program. `reaches_trans`, `reaches_step` and `reaches_final` compose the supported-prefix relation `PqxdhRecordLiftAvailability.Reaches` and show that a supported final result of a reached remainder is a supported final result of the whole run. `safe_of_reaches` turns a property of every reached query prestate into a query-safety certificate (`Availability.Safe`), and `safe_mono` weakens its predicate. `chain_lookup_present`, `lookup_present` and `present_preserved` show that a lookup populates its own coordinate and that a populated coordinate stays populated through every private service. `lookup_absent` combines them: if a coordinate is absent from every supported final table, no supported prefix reaches its lookup. ## The seed prefix and the adjacent policy

`complete_whole` shows that a completed first-allocation scan is a completed run of the whole client. `scan_absent` shows that every supported final state of the first-allocation scan of the frozen program has an empty table at every coordinate of the selected slot: at a pending cut by the source table provenance of `PqxdhRecordLiftSeedScope.pending_schedule`; at a completed run because the never-allocated slot has no seed (`GlobalSeedCut.scan_initial_missing`), while every table entry of the run's final state belongs to an allocated slot (`MixedWorld.protocol_run_good`, `TableScoped`). With `lookup_absent` and `run_agrees` this gives `seed_prefix`: the proof-attached first-allocation prefix is the same under the policy and under its update that idealizes one initial coordinate of the selected slot, because that coordinate is never looked up before the slot's first allocation. `uniform_policy` assembles the hop identification: for a policy that is real at the selected initial coordinate, `seedUniform` has the law of the adjacent concrete run followed by the same observer. On a completed prefix (the slot is never allocated) both sides are the same completed run and the extra draws are dropped. On a pending prefix, `independent_observed` (with `Good` from `SeedScope.sampled_program`) replaces the independent child by the adjacent policy for every value of the masked half, and `GlobalSeedCut.miss_continuation` reassembles the seed draw; `seed_prefix` and `GlobalSeedCut.resume_prefix` reassemble the prefix.

## Scope and accounting

All statements are operational equalities, invariants or contextual distribution laws. They add no cryptographic contract, no source reachability assumption, no byte inequality and no oracle-security premise. The view erases only the private native and uniform counters; the relay's child draw is outside the original uniform ledger, and the adjacent policy's ideal draw is counted by its own interpreter but erased by the view, so the enclosing bills count it (`PqxdhRecordLiftChainBills`). `different_source` instantiates `SeedRelay.unused_source` with the real production HKDF stream at the ratchet address of the restored half, the derivation that `GlobalSeedCut.interpreter_realization` uses; no random table replaces a public HKDF evaluation.
