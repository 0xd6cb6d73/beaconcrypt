## `PqxdhRecordLiftHiding` (55 guarded theorems)

`idealRecords_hiding` proves `HidingStatement setup codec route serverKid bounds n` as defined, for every setup, codec, router, Server key ID, fresh bounds and slot count: for every environment and mode-P adversary (the target has no `Frame` or budget premise) and every length-preserving `replace`, the hiding projection `hidingView (ctxView result)` has the same law in `ctxRun (fun _ => .random) replace ...` as in `idealRecords ... .P`. The equality is between the mapped computations themselves, so it holds for every event and every decision of the hiding projection (`PqxdhRecordLiftIdeal.hiding_event`, `hiding_advantage`). `hiding_replace` is the same statement for any two replacements that agree on lengths.

The proof is a syntactic coupling. `Coupled Rr Ro x y` (an inductive predicate) holds when the two computations make the same queries in the same order, replies related by `Rr` lead to related continuations and the final values are related by `Ro`. Four generic facts carry it: `Coupled.bind` and `Coupled.map`; `Coupled.refl` and `Coupled.refl_weighted` (a computation is coupled with itself when its replies are compared by equality, or when every query it can make has equal related replies, which `refl_noOpening` gets from the existing raw-cost bound `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxBills.handler_categories`); `Coupled.simulateQ_run` and `Coupled.simulateQ` (simulation through implementations whose single queries are coupled preserves couplings); and `Coupled.map_eq` (with equal coin replies, maps that agree on related final values give equal computations).

The world is then coupled with itself layer by layer, the replacing world on the left and the identity world on the right:

The facts behind the layers are: a random-mode seal reads only the plaintext's length, because its body and tag are fresh coins and its commitment is a lookup in the coordinate's private suffix dictionary, so with the same coins the record, the frame, the writer result and the state agree except for the plaintext recorded in the replay dictionary; replay acceptance compares nonce, associated data and encoded record, never the recorded plaintext, so openings succeed together and return the recorded (replaced or original) plaintext; the source receive's publication `finish` depends on the opening reply only through success (`normalFinish_couple`); the environment handler sees only registration effects; and the ledger's test-session check reads the transcript only through its mode-P view (`PqxdhDeploymentTranscript.designate_view`). `worldProgram_couple`, `program_couple` and `ctxRun_couple` assemble the layers, and `hidingView_rel` checks that the hiding projection reads only fields that agree on related results: the adversary's output, the transcript's mode-P view, the test session with its bit, the fresh messages, the environment state, the produced frames (equal base state), the raw counters and the handle-to-slot map (equal control state).
