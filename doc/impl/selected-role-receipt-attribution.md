This outer option cannot replace the returned plaintext with an invented value, and raw receives do not take the selected sealing-nonce stop. `beacon_accepts` shows that an accepted Beacon response came from its actual established phase and selected owner. It proves the final Beacon state is the original reinstallation of that owner's returned state; inactive phases cannot produce acceptance. `server_accepts` obtains the literal sender-header route, identity-derived associated-data lookup, repeated peer lookup and selected owner. These statements use the original executable role and family compilers.
