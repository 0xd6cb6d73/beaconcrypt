## The later worlds

`PqxdhRecordLiftLaterWorlds.chainsOf ideal` and `ctxRunOf modes replace` are the chain worlds and CTX worlds of `PqxdhRecordLiftIdeal` for an arbitrary adaptive World client of the frozen result shape (`Outcome`): the private interpreter of the lookup policy or of the CTX modes over the compiled client `PqxdhRecordLiftProtocol.schedule codec.frameRead route client initialControl`. At the frozen run-phase program they are the run-phase worlds by definition (`chains_eq`, `ctxRun_eq`), so nothing about the run-phase targets changes.

The later worlds are these worlds at the compiled two-phase program `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.program env crypto codec serverKid bounds mode reveal first second`: the frozen run phase of `first` (startup, the secrecy wrapper and its ledger), then `reveal` of the run-final environment state, then the post adversary `second output (reveal state)` through `postEmbed` and the same `native env`, all through the same World handler. `laterChains ideal` covers every lookup policy (`laterRealChains`, `laterIdealChains` at the two ends) and `laterCtxRun modes replace` every CTX mode assignment and plaintext map (`laterIdealAuth` all replay, `laterIdealRecords` replay in mode O and random in mode P, `laterRandomRecords` all random). This is the Protocol.schedule form that the plan asks for.

Identification with the existing later game: the all-real later world is `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.run` (`laterReal_source`) and gives `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.game` (`laterReal_game`). Split: for every private interpreter the compiled two-phase program is the compiled run-phase program followed by the E-only continuation `continueWith` (`schedule_split`), so every later chain hybrid is the run-phase hybrid of the same policy followed by the continuation (`laterChains_split`) and every later CTX world the run-phase CTX world of the same modes followed by it (`laterCtxRun_split`).

## The post phase is E-only

The compiled post phase of every post adversary requests only seed allocation, coins, shared hashes and charges (`post_schedule`, from `post_effects`: the post phase issues only restricted environment effects, and `effects_post`, `handler_post`, `schedule_post`). It never requests a chain lookup, a hidden opening or sealing, a caller-known record operation or a publication, so it reads no hidden key, nonce or chain, whatever the post adversary or the environment's HMAC handler does: an HMAC query of the post phase goes to the environment, whose state contains no seed (F2 by construction). Consequently the continuation is the same under every lookup policy (`continueWith_policy`), and in every CTX mode it is the chain-ideal continuation on the base private state with every CTX key state unchanged (`continueWith_ctx`). The post phase may install new hidden kernels through the environment's capabilities (seed allocation), but it derives no hidden coordinate, which is why every later target keeps the run-phase index set `hops n budget = (sends + 51 * receives) * (n * 2)`.

## How the proofs work

The two-phase program is a World client with the same record budget (`later_recordBounded`, from `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.program_record_bound`: the post phase makes no record request). So each later lift is proved by restating the wrappers for an arbitrary World client within the record budget (`RecordBounded`) and instantiating them at the two-phase program. No new hybrid argument is needed, and every per-index loss is the run-phase loss of an explicitly constructed adversary that runs the whole two-phase program, post phase included.

## Bills and the hash route

Chain: an observer of a compiled client whose services draw at most `bill` uniform bytes draws at most `2 * bill + 44 + cost` (`observerOf_total`). The two-phase World program stays within `laterProgramBill` in every component ledger (`later_interface_weighted`, from `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.program_effect_bound` with the run budget as the outer caps, and `program_record_bound`), its compiled form within `laterProgramCoins` uniform bytes (`later_weighted`, `later_coins`). `laterProgramCoins_closed`: the run-phase `PqxdhRecordLiftChainBills.programCoins` plus one byte per post coin effect and 64 per post hash and post administrative effect, where the post effects are `PqxdhRecordLiftLater.postCost` (direct post coins and hashes, and the environment's class-six allowance per post HMAC request). `laterObserverBill_closed`: the run-phase observer bill plus twice that post work.

CTX: the two-phase World program stays within `laterCtxProgramBill` in every CTX ledger (`later_world_categories`): the run-phase `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxBills.programBill` plus the post coin, hash and administrative effects at their per-effect bill. `laterCtxBills` (integrity) and `laterPrivBills` (privacy) charge, as their run-phase counterparts do, the program's coins plus the decision's coins plus 64 (integrity) or 66 (privacy) per shared-hash-ledger request, the program's shared hashes, one seal and the raw receives. The hash route: `laterCtx_hash_bill` and `laterPriv_hash_bill` show that the later hash bill is `laterSharedHashTotal`, the run-phase shared-hash total `sharedHashTotal` plus `postHash`, the post phase's shared-hash work (the `PostBudget` hash term `qHash₂` plus the class-six hash allowance per post HMAC request). This is the `qHash` of `integrityLoss`, so the `qHash / 2^128` term of every later integrity hop includes the post phase's hashes. `laterCtx_raw_bill`: the raw bill, the `qRaw` of `integrityLoss`, is the run-phase one.

- Seven modules instead of the single `PqxdhRecordLiftLaterLift` of plan S6: the worlds, the targets, one module per track (chain, CTX integrity, CTX privacy, hiding) and the composition. All names are under Claude's registered `PqxdhRecordLift*` prefix and were registered before creation.

- The S1 guide's decision D4 anticipated that the R6 CTX forms would need decisions with hash-oracle access. They do not: the post phase's hash requests are part of the compiled two-phase program, so the later CTX decisions stay cache-free, and the post hashes enter the hash bill instead (`laterCtx_hash_bill`). The D4 row of `doc/impl/record-lift-ideal.md` is updated accordingly.
- The later targets are new definitions in `PqxdhRecordLiftLaterTargets`; the frozen `PqxdhRecordLiftIdeal` is unchanged.
- The proofs restate the program-level wrappers of the chain, CTX and privacy tracks for an arbitrary World client instead of adding new hybrid arguments; this also makes the client-level bounds (`chain_loss_of`, `ctx_auth_loss_of`, `ctx_priv_loss_of`, `later_hiding_replace`) available for any World client within the record budget.
