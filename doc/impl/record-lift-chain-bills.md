# Query bills of the chain reductions

`PqxdhRecordLiftChainBills` proves that every explicit full 76-byte observer of the ordered chain hybrid (`PqxdhRecordLiftChainHybrid.observerAt`) makes at most `observerBill input = 2 * programCoins input + 44 + input.decision` uniform queries, for every environment within its `Frame`, every adversary within its `Budget` and every decision within its coin cost. `programCoins` is a closed expression in the fields of `PqxdhRecordLiftIdeal.BillInput`, so the bill is the function of the budget, the environment's `Frame` data, the fresh bounds and the decision cost that the target requires. It is stage C4 of the chain track (item 5, takeover plan §2 S3). No contract is used.

## The bill

`effectTotal input kind` is the number of restricted effects of one class (coin, hash, administrative) that the frozen program can issue: the startup coins, the direct adversary, test, fresh and public-hash work, and the environment's per-class allowance, exactly as in `PqxdhRecordLiftBudget.program_bound`. `programBill kind input` is the bill of the whole compiled program in one component ledger of `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxProtocolBounds` (uniform, hash, seal, raw): one uniform draw per coin effect, one 64-byte seed allocation per administrative effect, one shared-hash request per hash effect, and the per-record bills `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxProgramBounds.operationBill` per send (`sends`) and per raw receive (`receives`). `programCoins input = programBill .uniform input + 64 * programBill .hash input` counts uniform bytes: each shared-hash request may draw one 64-byte digest. In closed form (`programCoins_closed`), `programCoins = C_coin + 64 * C_hash + 64 * C_admin + 140 * sends + 3940 * receives`, with `C_kind = effectTotal input kind`: a send costs at most one lookup (76) and one sealing (64), a raw receive at most 51 lookups (3876) and one opening (64). `observerBill` adds the prefix and the suffix of a cut (each bounded by `programCoins`), the 44 retained bytes of a parent cut (32 for a seed cut) and the decision's coins; `chainBills` is the corresponding `ChainBills`.

## Proof

`weighted_total` transports a weighted structural bound (`PqxdhRecordLiftCtxProgramBounds.Weighted`) through a stateful probabilistic simulation whose every step is total-bounded by its cost; `weighted_of_total`, `total_of_weighted`, `weighted_cost_mono` and `weighted_scale` are its bookkeeping, and `countCoin_total` shows that the coin counter of `PqxdhRecordLiftWorld` issues exactly the original queries. `coinCost` is the per-service uniform-draw cost (allocation 64, lookup 76, coin 1, a public hash, hidden opening or sealing or caller-known operation 64, charge and publication 0), and `coinCost_categories` identifies it with the uniform ledger plus 64 times the hash ledger. `primitives_total` and `logged_total` bound a native program with at most `h` shared-hash calls by `64 * h` (`PqxdhRecordLiftWorldBudget.primitive_uniform_bound`, `PqxdhDeploymentCompileBudget.logged_bound`), and `open_hash` and `seal_hash` show that a hidden opening and a hidden sealing make at most one shared-hash call (`RawCtxSourceOpen.owner_primitive_bounds`, `RawCtxSourceSeal.sealInput_bound`); the caller-known operations use `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxProtocolBounds.known_send_hash` and `known_receive_hash`. With `allocation_total`, `lookup_total`, `sample_total` and `maskedLookup_total` this gives the per-service bounds `protocol_total` (the private Protocol interpreter under every mixed policy, from every private state), `mask_total` (the parent-masked interpreter of `PqxdhRecordLiftGlobalMask` under every supplied child) and `seedMask_total` (the seed-masked interpreter of `PqxdhRecordLiftGlobalSeedCut` under every supplied child). The masked bounds are proved directly, not through realization, so they hold for an arbitrary child.

`effectBill`, `worldBill`, `resolve_weighted`, `charge_free`, `effects_weighted`, `ownerBill`, `ownerRun_weighted` and `handler_weighted` bill every world request of the compiled client in every component ledger, for every control state (records through `PqxdhRecordLiftCtxProgramBounds.kernel_send` and `kernel_receive`). `worldBill_classes`, `interface_weighted`, `program_weighted` and `program_coins` bound the frozen interface program and the compiled private-service program with the static caps of `PqxdhRecordLiftBudget.program_bound` and `program_record_bound`. `lookupScan_weighted`, `lookupScan_pending`, `seedScan_weighted` and `seedScan_pending` show that suspending the program at a first lookup or a first allocation cannot increase its bill, and that the suspended continuation of every supported pending cut inherits it. `parentObserver_total` and `seedObserver_total` combine these with `PqxdhRecordLiftGlobalCut.reductionObserver_bound` and `PqxdhRecordLiftGlobalSeedCut.reductionObserver_bound`, and `observerAt_total` gives the bill of every index.

## Scope

The bills are static, all-reply bounds: they hold on every path of every observer, including completed prefixes, failed native operations and rejected input, and for every supplied child. The mixed policy's ideal draws are counted as lookups (76 bytes each), so the cap covers every hybrid and both endpoints. The HMAC challenge-query bills of the HMAC-PRF games (two per observer) are the existing `reduction_bounds` of the cut modules. Program-level transports of the other component ledgers (`program_weighted` for `.hash`, `.seal`, `.raw`) are available to the CTX track.
