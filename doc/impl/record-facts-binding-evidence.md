# Actual shared-cache evidence for record binding

`PqxdhRecordFactsBindingEvidence` proves 27 statements about actual caller-known record operations and the shared random-oracle cache. A successful caller-known send or receive supplies an actual digest already stored in that cache, and every later service, native command and full-ledger continuation retains the witness. These are source facts needed by the cross-handle binding argument. They do not assert the global `BindingStatement` or assume that the selected handle is hidden or honest.

`SharedCommit frame cache` means that the frame payload decodes to a record whose commitment bytes equal a 64-byte digest stored at some actual shared-ROM input. It does not assert that the input is secret, unique, or generated by an honest party. The input exists because the actual source called the hash oracle. `hash_cached`, `native_hash_cached`, `record_hash_run` and `record_hash_cached` expose that fact through the real uniform/native instrumentation and frame logger. `native_writer_reply` and `record_writer_reply` establish that the actual codec reply is forwarded unchanged.

`source_send_cached` follows the entire source send through KDF, AEAD sealing, commitment hashing and frame writing. It uses the existing `FrameReadBack` codec contract to connect successful returned ciphertext bytes to the exact submitted frame. Its public service wrapper is `known_send_cached`. `source_receive_cached` follows the complete source receive through the actual selected cached or future key, parsing, commitment comparison and AEAD opening. The hash reply must equal the parsed commitment before the returned plaintext can be accepted; `known_receive_cached` carries that witness to the actual private service poststate. The receive theorem takes the existing typed `OpenContext`; the outer source routing proof still has to connect its frame to the raw bytes supplied by the adversary. No secretly recovered plaintext is inserted into an adversary input.

`hash_grows`, `deployment_cache_grows`, `native_cache_grows`, `native_program_cache_grows`, `record_cache_grows` and `record_program_cache_grows` retain prior shared answers through the actual counted native and logged interfaces. `SharedCommit.grows` transports a concrete frame witness along that cache order. `hashImpl_grows` and `hash_program_grows` cover interleaved hash and finite-coin requests, while `keyed_cache_grows` and `keyed_program_cache_grows` cover the complete hidden CTX computation in every mode, including real mode.

`service_cache_grows` checks every actual private-service constructor. It includes allocations and their sampler, public hashes, explicit charges, chain lookups, hidden openings and seals, caller-known sends and receives, and writer publication. Lookup hits and misses retain the shared cache while preserving their actual KDF and uniform instrumentation. Failed parsing, writer refusal, failed authentication and rejected source operations remain in the supported execution. The proof does not replace any of these operations with a free oracle or a state-free abstraction.

`program_cache_grows`, `native_query_cache_grows`, `native_schedule_cache_grows`, `full_cache_grows` and `full_program_cache_grows` extend the checked service fact through complete adaptive source continuations. They cover raw delivery, honest record requests, reveals, tests, arbitrary environment commands and stopping through the existing compiled interfaces. These statements hold from arbitrary private initial states and for arbitrary key-mode assignments; no freshness, budget, honesty or additional primitive premise is introduced. They preserve earlier evidence regardless of whether a matching publication occurs before or after a receive.

All 27 statements have exact guarded axiom checks allowing only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This stage adds no probabilistic reduction or query bound. All native, uniform and effect counters stay in the original supported computation, and their source and reduction bills must be supplied by the separate accounting join. The remaining binding proof must combine actual known-path evidence with the hidden dictionary witnesses, retain handle ownership through the final transcript, derive a concrete distinct-input cache collision, and connect that event to the checked statistical oracle compiler and its global query caps. No Rust, wire format, primitive assumption, snapshot or deployment behavior changes here.
