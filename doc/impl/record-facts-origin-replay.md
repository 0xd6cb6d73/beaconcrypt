# Exact ideal-record replay witnesses

`PqxdhRecordFactsOriginReplay` supplies local witnesses for the pending `PqxdhRecordLiftIdeal.OriginStatement` proof. It does not prove that complete world statement or a publication probability bound.

The replay dictionary compares the nonce, the full `RecordAD`, and the complete encoded record. `RecordAD` includes the sequence and sender outside its 153 associated-data bytes. `replay_witness` recovers the actual earlier dictionary entry and its exact plaintext. `normalized_record`, `target_matches`, and `matches_selected` show that the accepted payload parser and these full context fields recover the entire source frame: sequence, sender, and every payload byte. They need no assumption that different symbolic constructors are injective.

`localPrivate_seals` shows that a private suffix lookup preserves the seal dictionary, including a lookup before any seal. `localRaw_accepted` preserves the actual optional replay plaintext and the seal dictionary. `keyRaw_witness` applies these facts to either replay or random key mode; its explicit `mode ≠ .real` premise is necessary. No acceptance-to-dictionary conclusion is claimed for real opening mode.

`decoder_frame` inverts the actual universal decoder on its packed envelope. The envelope retains the original raw bytes, selected nonce, and associated data; it supplies no message key or proposed plaintext. `keyRaw_frame` combines this inversion with the actual key interpreter: every supported successful raw reply in an ideal mode has a decoded frame, an existing dictionary entry matching that frame, and the entry's exact plaintext.

These nine statements are operational support facts. They use no security advantage, distribution replacement, freshness assumption, selection loss, or query-bound assumption. They introduce no primitive or codec contract. The module reaches primitive record fields through the imported interpreter definitions, but does not establish primitive security. The global dictionary-to-produced-frame join is now proved by [OriginInvariant](record-facts-origin-invariant.md). The accepted-receive join must still identify the opposite-role counterpart, and failed writers must be accounted for before concluding publication origin. Private suffix cache entries populated by earlier rejected raw calls must remain correlated throughout those joins.
