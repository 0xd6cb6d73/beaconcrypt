# Live record receive failure state and callback caps

`PqxdhBoundaryRecordReceiveState` consumes the exact receive execution boundary with seven guarded statements. It applies to the ordinary live record receive path. Failed registration receives and their separate query costs remain in the CE/registration lane; this increment makes no initial-registration budget claim.

`owner_interpretation` equates the complete interpreted receive transcription with the original raw owner game, retaining its actual optional plaintext, entire owner and entire primitive state. It works for an arbitrary stateful native interpreter. Its only representation input identifies the stored associated-data bytes with the actual fixed-size source array. `owner_supported` transports each completed normal result to that same full-state execution, without inventing a plaintext or changing raw input.

`owner_failure` proves that an actual failed receive restores the entire owned protocol state, including both chains, cached keys, routing and role metadata, while incrementing the raw-attempt counter once. It deliberately retains the actual separate primitive poststate. Failed commitment checks or opening may leave real shared hash-cache effects; no primitive/cache rollback is asserted. `owner_metadata` proves that every completed raw receive increments once and preserves the pinned sender, associated data and metadata, whether it succeeds or fails.

`owner_bounds`, `server_bounds` and `beacon_bounds` carry the original source bounds through the exact executor and role wrappers. Every raw receive has at most 51 KDF callbacks, at most one hash callback, at most one AEAD opening, and no sealing or writer callback. Missing peers, inactive phases, parser rejection, cached receive, future receive and opening failure are included by the underlying complete source drivers. These are native callback caps; they are not uniform/base-oracle totals or claims that decoding, copying, lookup or primitive computation is free.
