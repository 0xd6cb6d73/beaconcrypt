Its `Grows` relation says that the produced log after execution is the earlier log followed by a suffix and that every earlier successful-seal dictionary entry remains in the same coordinate dictionary. These are analysis logs, not persistent snapshots or restored secret state.

Publication appends the actual pending frames with their actual tag. It retains the entire earlier produced prefix, including frames published before a later receive or after an earlier receive.

The four remaining statements express exact retention as growth, reflexivity, transitivity through the actual intermediate state and membership retention for a previously produced frame.

The execution statements require every hidden coordinate mode to be either the existing integrity ideal or confidentiality ideal; they do not assert dictionary growth for real modes. The starting private state, source control, environment, plaintext replacement and adversary program are otherwise arbitrary. There is no honest-target premise, additional primitive or codec contract, assumed security conclusion, or marginal-distribution substitution. The theorem leaves the complete original cache, chain, seed and counter effects in the executed computation. It makes no claim that those operations are free and adds no draws, probability bound or query-bound conclusion. It supplies witness transport for the global origin and binding proofs; it does not itself establish their final events.
