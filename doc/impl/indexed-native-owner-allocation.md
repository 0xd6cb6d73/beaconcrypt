# Indexed native owner allocation

`PqxdhLiveIndexedCreation` connects the actual identity/prekey/KEM constructor to native owner publication. The executable allocator keeps the identity and KEM service indices inside the complete Fresh role and returns only the separate native-owner index and public identity. `create_source` equates the full operation, including both private service histories and the entire prior native owner list, with the literal source generator order. It does not move a KEM draw across the prekey generator or a later continuation.

`install_valid` preserves every older owner and establishes both the exact joint seed/public identity relation and the indexed KEM relation for the new role. `create_valid` obtains those facts for every supported full source allocation result. `empty_valid` provides the initial owner-list base case. `allocated_lookup` derives the returned native-owner slot from the actual append; it never equates the native, identity-service and KEM-service indices, or assumes public encoding injectivity.

`create_uniform` retains the complete source cost of 32 identity-byte draws plus qPrekey plus qKeygen, where the latter two bounds cover the complete interpreted original generators. `create_bills` separately counts one joint creation, one KEM creation and qPrekey local uniform requests. Publishing the owner adds no request. These are exact operational and resource facts, not a secrecy/authentication theorem or a claim that arbitrary supplied private histories are reachable. The global compiler must still preserve these relations through Phase1, registration, raw traffic and actual retained-field reveals, and construct the selected-generation reduction with its explicit selection loss. Erased fields cannot be revealed merely because a proof interpreter retains an analysis table. Primitive internals, snapshots and persistence stay outside this model.
