# Compiling the saved receive remainder

`RawCtxSelectedPendingReceiveCompiler` connects a receive saved at a private derivation to the ordinary dynamic CTX interface. Its executable `effect` runs the existing `RawCtxInterpretedReceive.program` from the saved effect. Its `resume` supplies an already obtained predecessor response once, preserving the original entry, working counter, skipped slots, target and raw context. It does not repeat the parser, admission, or earlier derivations.

`effect_source` preserves the complete opaque ratchet result, actual optional plaintext, and shared source cache. Its opening conditions are the original nonempty raw input, exact decoder reply, valid frame and reached target/context. `native_effect` discharges the derivation and opening simulation obligations with the actual retained private table and native backend. `native_effect_source` combines these equalities without a successful-opening, honest-ciphertext, freshness, or callback-equivalence premise. The native interpreter still executes the real known-key cryptographic operations and preserves every failure and rollback outcome.

`known_resume` and `resume_source` preserve the entire existing state and supplied predecessor material as known values. The selected child message key is introduced only if a later private derivation reaches its selected address. The existing private-table compiler retains predecessor priority, including address collisions. Known bytes that happen to equal the selected key remain known; no injectivity or absence-of-aliases premise is added.

`owner_resume_source` consumes `RawCtxForwardOwnerScope.Valid` on the literal saved owner descriptor. The preceding saved-descriptor scope milestone derives that fact from the actual annotated owner, role, Post and Builder programs and the complete prepared/material samplers. `owner_parent_source` specializes the resumed native source operation to the full 76-byte predecessor response, decoding its retained material and child-chain projection without an additional random draw. The theorem treats that response as supplied to the pending operation. The complete descriptor caller must still connect it to the selected source cut and retain the original installation-origin trace.

`effect_services` counts private-table lookups and opening callbacks separately under every typed reply. The private lookup allowance is the exact remaining source counter cost; the opening allowance is one. `effect_queries` and `resume_queries` give at most one dynamic CTX call. `resume_prior` charges at most `max(1, pending.remaining) - 1` further private lookups after consuming the supplied response once. These are logical component and private-lookup bounds, not a claim that their native implementation, parser, local computation, or prefix work is free. Known-key hash/AEAD costs and complete outer callback costs retain their separate component/source ledgers.

The executable compiler has no selected-message-key parameter. Its mathematical source and CTX evaluations do receive that key. These equations do not prove that arbitrary prior tables, contexts, closures or cached values are independent of it. The complete source sampler, freshness and disclosure arguments must establish the applicable nonexposure event. This module also does not yet publish the resumed owner into the saved outer role/Post/Builder frame, bound selected nonce refusal, or prove the final multi-session security statement.
