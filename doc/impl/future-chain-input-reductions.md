The statistical term is a finite-image bound, not a residual-secret entropy claim. The separate comparison challenger samples a complete independent 76-byte output after the full context. The observer is explicitly given all 76 bytes, including message key32, next chain32 and nonce12; it may adaptively choose every candidate after observing all of them. Hence even an observer knowing all output coordinates accepts with probability at most `2^-352` in this comparison game. Nonce or next-chain exposure does not enlarge the image. The actual game pays the exact constructed Extract and HMAC tester advantages to reach this comparison; the theorem makes no assertion that these advantages are small for an arbitrary supplied context.
